# Fixes and angle

## Evidence (honest sample size)

Amboras launched in 2026 (YC S26). A web search on 2026-11-19 found **no independent user reviews** — only the company's own
pages and aggregator listings. So there is no "what users hate" list here; nothing was invented to fill one.

One third-party critique was found:
- ecomm.design, "Best Amboras AI Alternatives" — https://ecomm.design/amboras-ai-alternatives/ — says Basic is $49/mo
  *plus a platform fee per sale*, that the agentic testing is only on Pro, and recommends alternatives with easier export
  for merchants who want control. (Note: Amboras's own FAQ says it charges no transaction fee; the two sources disagree.
  Unverified either way.)

The rest of the plan comes from the owner's own requirements: a single-owner platform to run their own store, no monthly
fee, invisible to shoppers.

## What is missing / what to fix (for this owner)

| # | fix | size | why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | White-label storefront: no platform name anywhere a shopper can see | S | owner requirement |
| 2 | Every feature unlocked, no plans, no per-sale fee; "Costs" page shows only real running costs | S | owner requirement; critique above |
| 3 | Full backup (JSON) + import, CSV export of orders/products | S | lock-in critique above |
| 4 | "Today" brief: the dashboard leads with what needs doing, ranked | M | turn the assistant from chat into a to-do list |
| 5 | Homepage section editor: reorder, hide, edit announcement / story / FAQ | M | direct control without prompting |
| 6 | Command palette (⌘K / Ctrl+K) for every page and action | S | speed for a single power user |

## Angle

For a merchant who wants an AI-run store without renting one, **Wharf** is a store platform you own outright:
every feature on, no monthly plan, no cut of your sales, your data exportable in one click, and nothing on the storefront
that says who built it.
