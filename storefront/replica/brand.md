# Brand: Tillery

## Name

**Tillery** — from *till* (a shop's cash register) + *-ery* (a place where something is made: bakery, winery). A place where
shops are made and run. Short, easy to say and spell. Not similar to Amboras. Replaced "Wharf" at the owner's request.

## Palette (tokens in `app/globals.css`, checked in `design/tokens.json`)

Sea green accent `#0d7a63` on warm white `#fbfaf7`, sand surfaces, deep sea ink `#14201d`, a buoy-orange highlight
`#c2410c` used sparingly. The old placeholder indigo `#4338ca` is retired (listed in brand.json so the sweep catches it).
The original's colours were never observed (text-only recon), so there is nothing of theirs to avoid.

## Logo brief

- Idea: a till seen from the front — counter line over an open cash drawer with a coin slot.
- Mark: symbol in a rounded square + wordmark "tillery" in a heavy grotesk, lowercase.
- Works at 16px (symbol only). Current SVG in `components/Logo.tsx` is a working draft, not final art.
- Must not resemble any commerce platform's bag/cart mark.

## Voice: plain, confident, warm

- Plain, not bland. Confident, not hype. Warm, not cute.
- Do: "Ship 3 orders." Don't: "Unlock your fulfilment potential!"
- Do: "This costs $0. Stripe takes 2.9% + 30¢." Don't: "Unbeatable value!"
- Do: "Nothing here says Wharf to your customers." Don't: "Seamless white-label experience."
- Do: "We couldn't charge that card." Don't: "Oops! Something went wrong 😅"
- Do: second person, short sentences, real numbers.
