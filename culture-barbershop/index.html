About the shop

A neighborhood shop that outgrew its chairs.

Culture started on 4th Street in LaSalle as a small shop with a loyal crowd. Word got around, the books filled up, and the waitlist kept growing.

So we moved. The new shop at 415 5th St in Peru has more room, more barbers and the same crew. Come in for a clean fade, a sharp lineup or a beard that finally behaves. Bring the kids too.
